Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 24, 2025

Privacy First: Run Chart Visualization is designed with your privacy as our top priority. We collect zero personal data and operate completely offline.

Introduction

Run Chart Visualization ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in our desktop application available through the Microsoft Store.

Information Collection

We collect ZERO personal information.

  • No personal data is collected, stored, or transmitted
  • No user accounts or registration required
  • No analytics or tracking of any kind
  • No network communications for data collection
  • All chart data and settings remain completely local to your device

Data Storage

All application data is stored locally on your Windows device:

  • Chart Data: Production numbers, goals, and specifications you enter
  • Application Settings: Your preferences for chart appearance and behavior
  • Local Only: No data is ever sent to external servers or cloud services
  • Your Control: You can clear all data by uninstalling the application

Application Permissions

Our application requires minimal Windows permissions:

  • File System Access: Only when you choose to save or export charts
  • Print Access: Only when you choose to print charts
  • No Network Access: The app works completely offline

Data Processing

All statistical calculations and chart generation occur locally on your device:

  • Run chart calculations are performed in real-time on your device
  • Special cause detection algorithms run locally
  • No data is sent to external services for processing
  • Chart images are generated locally when you choose to save or print

Third-Party Services

We use NO third-party services that collect data.

  • No advertising networks
  • No analytics services
  • No cloud storage providers
  • No social media integrations

Microsoft Store Integration

This application is distributed through the Microsoft Store:

  • Microsoft Store may collect standard app installation/update metrics
  • We do not have access to any Microsoft Store user data
  • App ratings and reviews are handled entirely by Microsoft
  • For Microsoft's privacy practices, see their Privacy Statement

Children's Privacy

Our application is safe for all ages:

  • No personal information is requested from users of any age
  • No online features or communications
  • Completely educational and professional tool

Data Security

Since no data leaves your device:

  • Your data is as secure as your Windows device
  • No risk of data breaches from external servers
  • No network transmission of sensitive information
  • Standard Windows file system security applies

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. We will post any changes on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Your Rights

Since we collect no personal data:

  • There is no personal data to access, modify, or delete
  • No data portability concerns
  • Complete control over your local application data
